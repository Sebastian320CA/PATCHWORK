# Haunted Atlas: product rules

Kiroween 2026 entry, category **Frankenstein**. Deadline: 4 Dec 2026, 2:00 pm Pacific.

**What it is:** point a phone camera at a printed page of a book. The app reads the
page in the browser (OCR), finds the real places in it, flies a map to them, and narrates
the passage. Threads ("stitches") connect words on the page to their place on the map.

**Demo book:** *Dracula* (Bram Stoker), an old public-domain edition only.

## Non-negotiable rules
- **The demo path must not depend on the internet or on paid services.** Judges test for free until 29 Jan 2027. Use bundled data (place list, city list) instead of live APIs.
- **Public-domain or openly licensed content only** (books, images, fonts, map data). Credit every source in `docs/CREDITS.md`.
- **Everything runs in the browser.** No server, no accounts, no API keys. User pages never leave their device.
- **All user-facing text and project docs are in English** (the submission requires it).
- **Degrade gracefully:** no camera, no speech support, or bad OCR must never leave a blank screen. Offer upload and show the text.
- **Keep scope small.** Minimum: camera or photo, OCR, place detection, map, voice, one prepared book. Anything else is optional.
